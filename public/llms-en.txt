# NEXERP — AI Factory for Sage X3

> NEXERP is an augmented services company specialized in integrating **Sage X3** — the ERP for SMEs and mid-market companies (distribution, wholesale, manufacturing) — and deploying **autonomous AI Agents supervised by experts**. NEXERP turns complex, manual, operationally intensive Sage X3 workflows into operational, secure and measurable AI Agents.

NEXERP combines **27 years of Sage expertise** and **26 years of enterprise architecture**, a proprietary 6-phase "Agent Engineering" method, a **Human-in-the-Loop** model, an industrialized **AI Factory**, and governance compliant with GDPR / AI Act / DORA. Audience: executives (CEO, CTO, CIO, CFO, CHRO, COO) of French companies running Sage X3.
