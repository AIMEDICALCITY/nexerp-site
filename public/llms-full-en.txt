# NEXERP — AI Factory for Sage X3

> NEXERP (nexerp.fr) is an augmented services company specialized in integrating **Sage X3**, the ERP for SMEs and mid-market companies — distribution, wholesale and manufacturing — and in deploying **autonomous, governed AI Agents validated by your business owners** (Human-in-the-Loop model). Our AI Factory configures agents to reduce operational pain points and orchestrate medium-to-complex Sage X3 management workflows.

Core message: **"Our expertise is AI orchestration architecture, not ERP expertise: Sage X3 knowledge lives in the business agentic RAG, and sensitive decisions stay with your teams."**

---

## Who
Executives and managers of French companies already running Sage X3: CEO, CTO, CIO, CFO, CHRO, COO, business directors and managers. Organizations facing growing operational complexity, manual workflows, documentation debt, and excessive dependence on internal experts or vendors.

## What
Sage X3 operates in a shifting regulatory and operational context. Workflows grow complex (configuration, integrations, operations, bugs, business exceptions), many activities remain manual and dependent on key people, repetitive tasks consume expert time, documentation is hard to use, and knowledge transfer is fragile. AI initiatives, the AI Factory and AI Agents provide a concrete answer.

## How
NEXERP offers an AI transformation program. We co-develop, with our clients, AI Agents specific to Sage X3 workflows. Agents are configured, tested, supervised and validated by your business owners. They rely on Skills Systems, Memory, User, Soul and Context. Deployment follows a Human-in-the-Loop, AgentOps, security, compliance and ROI-measurement logic. Knowledge is capitalized through `memory.md`.

## Where
Clients based in France. Remote, hybrid or on-site engagement depending on the phase.

## Why
Properly configured, trained and governed AI Agents solve simple, medium and complex Sage X3 workflow problems: improved operational efficiency, faster execution, knowledge documentation, secured operations, and increased business-team capacity.

## Proprietary method — Agent Engineering (6 phases)
1. **Item Data & Master Records** — item master enrichment, reference-data consistency checks, duplicate detection.
2. **Sales & Sales Administration** — order checks, price consistency, delivery and invoicing follow-up.
3. **Purchasing & Procurement** — supplier validation, order tracking, lead-time and shortage alerts.
4. **Stock & Inventory** — multi-site control, inventory anomaly detection, valuation.
5. **Production & MRP** — bill-of-material and routing consistency, work-order tracking, planning alerts.
6. **Quality & Traceability** — quality controls, non-conformity handling, upstream/downstream lot traceability.
7. **Supply Chain & Logistics** — flow steering, picking and shipping, WMS/carrier interfaces.
8. **Finance & Accounting** — reconciliations, journal-entry controls, closings, group reporting.
9. **Management Control & Budgets** — budget tracking, variance analysis, forecasting.
10. **CRM & Customer Relationship** — segmentation, opportunity tracking, scoring and follow-ups.
11. **Project Management** — progress tracking, cost allocation, drift alerts.
12. **Maintenance & Equipment** — maintenance plans, work orders, equipment availability.
13. **Multi-Company & Multi-Site** — inter-company consolidation, intercompany flows, group reporting.
14. **Users, Rights & Security** — permission audits, abnormal-access detection, periodic rights review.
15. **Reporting & Business Intelligence** — automated report generation, weak-signal detection, business alerts.
16. **Configuration & Environments** — configuration documentation, consistency control, regression testing.
17. **Integrations, EDI & Web Services** — interface monitoring, failure detection, technical documentation.

## Packaged offers
- **Sage X3 & AI Diagnostic — 4 weeks.** Rapid audit of pain points, friction, manual workflows, recurring bugs, business dependencies and automation opportunities. Deliverable: high-stakes workflow mapping + automation backlog + AI Readiness score + 90-day roadmap.
- **Automation Sprint — 8 to 12 weeks.** Build a first AI Agent on a priority workflow, under business-owner control. Deliverable: tested and validated agentic prototype/MVP, Sage X3 expert review, immediate ROI measurement.
- **Sage X3 AI Factory — 6 months.** Set up an industrial foundation: skills catalog, AgentOps, security, monitoring, governance, prompt templates, memory system. Deliverable: reusable platform for multiple agents.
- **Run & AgentOps — monthly subscription.** Supervision, maintenance, evaluation, continuous improvement, token-cost tracking, error and incident monitoring. Deliverable: agents in production with SLA, dashboard and reporting.
